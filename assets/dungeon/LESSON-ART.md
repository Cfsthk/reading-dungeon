# Dungeon lesson and mechanism artwork

The built-in ImageGen tool produced the four transparent source sheets with `annex-items.png` as the visual reference. The production sheets preserve alpha and use nearest-neighbour resampling to pack the visible art into regular cells. Each cell has a one-pixel transparent gutter.

| Production file | Layout | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| lesson-bases.png | 4 × 2 | Stone seal, tide marker, spore jar, iron mould, library seal, ice marker, wax jar and astronomical plate |
| lesson-mounts.png | 2 × 2 | Flush inlay, projecting metal claws, cord wrapped around the body, cord tied at the neck |
| lesson-patterns.png | 4 × 4 | Crossed and parallel lines, alternating and grouped shades, symmetric and asymmetric motifs, four combined line/symmetry contrasts, spare motifs |
| mechanisms.png | 3 × 3 | Two sluice positions and channel, cart and rail and buffer, two drum positions and striker |

The game places the attachment, pattern and coloured rune independently on a common regional base. The mechanism variants reuse the existing rune medallions, gold ribbon and lantern from the dungeon atlases. Every mechanism has two intact lamp housings. Lit glass glows amber and the unlit glass is shaded slate blue. Selecting a working sluice extends the stream from its stone outlet. The cart rolls up its own siding. The drum is struck, turns its face and emits a signal toward the exit. Each has a shorter reduced-motion treatment.
