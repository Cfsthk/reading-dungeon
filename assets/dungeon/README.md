## Revision 10 — entrance and four new areas

Flat north walls accept a variable number of doors. Chasm and ledge backdrops accept modular bridges and ladders. The game draws the explorer and clickable objects separately.

The file names below are relative to this folder. The five background PNGs use `rooms-a.png` as a pixel-art style reference.

## Revision 9 — trap reactions

The reactions are native Canvas animations of the existing transparent pixel-art atlases. The selected box keeps its wood or iron body and ribbon as the lid opens: treasure reveals the existing coin sprite, while a mimic reveals a pixel mouth, teeth and eyes before biting. A wrong person lunges from the chosen alcove; other objects discharge a small curse effect. Broken planks and rungs are cut from the matching intact crossing sprite. The bridge fall shrinks the explorer into the chasm; a ladder fall drops the explorer toward the lower floor. These reaction overlays and transforms were authored in code; no new generated atlas is required.
