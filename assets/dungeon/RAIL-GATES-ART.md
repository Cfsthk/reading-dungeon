# Rail crossing and gate artwork

The transparent production atlas `rail-gates.png` holds eight 256 × 192 cells. The top row contains the side-profile ore cart, horizontal rails and matching right and left arrow boards. The second row contains two-leaf wooden, stone and iron doors and the wheel. The renderer fits each piece to its own proportions and animates the two leaves about their outside jambs. The four original doorway frames, torches, cracks and runes are retained.

ImageGen produced the source sheets using the existing mechanisms as the style reference. Each sprite was trimmed to its alpha bounds and packed using nearest-neighbour sampling. The left-facing board mirrors the right-facing board. The runtime repeats the central portion of the rail sprite so the steel rail continues through each join. Background-specific endpoints keep the track between the dungeon walls.
