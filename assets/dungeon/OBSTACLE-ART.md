# Blocked passage artwork

Two transparent game atlases combine three sheets produced by ImageGen. The existing `mechanisms.png` supplied the camera angle, lighting and pixel rendering reference. The packed game assets use nearest-neighbour sampling and retain their source transparency. Matched empty and full basins, red and blue lever poses, and the three beast poses remain distinct sprites.

| Runtime file | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `obstacle-props.png` | Sleeping, listening and walking beast, silver fish, bell, red and blue bowls, empty and filled basins and four lever poses |
| `obstacle-terrain.png` | Continuous wet and drained channel textures, exposed stepping stone and wooden feeding ledge |

The river textures join mirrored segments at the banks. Each dungeon backdrop has its own channel width to keep the water inside the wall and bookcase edges. The explorer places the fish into the selected coloured bowl. A successful selection slides that bowl onto the side ledge and the beast moves to it. The inactive levers stand upright and a pulled lever leans left.
